4. Location Data

4.1 How We Use Location Data

Location data is critical to the core functionality of the vDrive Driver App:

  • Foreground Location: Used when you are actively using the app to show your position on the map, match you with nearby ride requests, and provide navigation.
  • Background Location: Used when you are online (available for rides) but the app is minimized, to continue matching you with ride requests, track active trips, and share your live location with passengers for safety.

4.2 When Location Is Collected

ScenarioLocation TypePurpose
App open, driver onlineForegroundRide matching, navigation
App minimized, driver onlineBackgroundContinuous ride matching
Active trip in progressForeground + BackgroundTrip tracking, passenger safety, route logging
App closed or driver offlineNot collected—

4.3 Your Control

You can stop location sharing at any time by going offline within the app. Background location collection stops immediately when you go offline or close the app.

7. Data Storage & Security

7.1 Storage

  • Your data is stored on secure servers hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) and other reputable cloud providers.
  • Sensitive data such as authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).
  • Local data persistence uses encrypted AsyncStorage for app state management.

7.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your data:

  • Encryption: Data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols
  • Access Control: Role-based access controls limit data access to authorized personnel only
  • Token-Based Authentication: Secure JWT-based authentication with automatic token refresh
  • Secure Storage: Sensitive credentials stored using platform-native secure storage mechanisms
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and vulnerability testing

7.3 Data Breach

In the event of a data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required under the DPDPA 2023 and other applicable laws.
