## 6. Trip Lifecycle & Cancellation

### 6.1 Trip Flow
A typical trip follows this lifecycle:
1. **Ride Request**: You receive a ride request notification
2. **Accept / Skip**: You accept the ride or skip it
3. **Navigate to Pickup**: You navigate to the passenger's pickup location
4. **Arriving / Arrived**: You mark your arrival status
5. **Pickup Verification**: OTP verification with the passenger (if applicable)
6. **Trip In Progress**: You transport the passenger to the destination
7. **Destination Reached**: You mark the destination as reached
8. **Payment Collection**: Fare is collected (cash or digital)
9. **Trip Complete**: The trip is marked as complete

### 6.2 Cancellation Policy
- You may cancel a trip before pickup for valid reasons (e.g., passenger not found, unsafe conditions, vehicle breakdown)
- Excessive cancellations may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent deactivation
- Cancellation reasons must be provided honestly through the App

### 6.3 Scheduled Rides

- Failure to fulfil scheduled rides without valid reason may result in penalties
